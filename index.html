How to play

Pull up a chair. You share one screen, one case file, and one decision at the end of each chapter.

  1. Open the exhibits.Read the statements aloud. Study the maps, objects, and records together. Every clue you need is in the file.
  2. Trade observations.Let one player read while another keeps notes. Take turns navigating, checking distances, and questioning each other’s theory.
  3. Record your finding.Agree on an answer, then submit it. You can revisit earlier exhibits, ask for a hint, and revise a finding as often as you like.

Your case and notebook save in this browser. Take as long as you need.
